Privacy Policy

Hidden Facts Publisher · last updated 25 November 2026

Who uses this app

Hidden Facts Publisher is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Hidden Facts తెలుగు (@hiddenfacts_te) to manage that channel. It is not available to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's Google sign-in, the app accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API: uploading and editing the channel's videos, thumbnails, captions, playlists and comments, and reading the channel's own analytics.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens and analytics data are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to publish the channel's content and plan future videos. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app does not collect data from viewers or any other Google users.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: contact the channel through its YouTube About page.
